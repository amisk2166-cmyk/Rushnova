Rushnova Privacy Policy

Rushnova uses your email and player name to manage your account, match registrations, wallet balances, payment requests and support. Transaction records may be stored to maintain wallet accuracy and prevent misuse.

Never share your password, OTP, payment PIN or private account credentials. Payment credentials and server secrets should not be displayed in the website. Your email is shown only in your signed-in profile area.

Wallet and payment information is used to process the features you request, investigate disputed transactions and maintain service security. To request help with account data, contact Rushnova support through the official support channel listed on the website.

This is an initial site policy template; the operator should review it against the actual data practices before public launch.

Rushnova Terms & Conditions

  1. Use one genuine account and accurate player details. Do not exploit bugs, cheat, collude or disrupt matches.
  2. Level requirement: only Free Fire players at level 40 or above are eligible to enter a match. A player found below level 40 may be removed from the match by the organiser. Entry eligibility should be checked before joining.
  3. Entry fees, prizes and match timing are shown on the relevant match card. Do not join unless you understand the displayed terms.
  4. Wallet credits and withdrawals are subject to payment verification and review. Never treat a pending payment as confirmed.
  5. Room ID/password should not be shared publicly. Follow admin instructions and fair-play rules.
  6. By using Rushnova, you agree to follow these rules and applicable laws. Contact support for disputes.

Important: this profile page displays the level rule; server-side match enforcement must also be implemented in the join/registration backend before it can be guaranteed.
